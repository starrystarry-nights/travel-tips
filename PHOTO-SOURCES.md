## Public PWA privacy check

The 13 JPEG files synchronized for the public PWA were visually inspected: they show landscape/editorial travel imagery, not booking screenshots, documents or contact details. Before upload, EXIF, XMP, IPTC and JPEG comments were removed losslessly. Decoded pixel hashes were checked unchanged. No personal user uploads or IndexedDB content are included.
